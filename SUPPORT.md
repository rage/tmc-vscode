# Support

To receive help with an exercise, you can contact the course staff or use the support channels found in the course materials.

For problems with the extension, read the [FAQ](docs/FAQ.md) and the [troubleshooting page](docs/troubleshooting.md) first. If they do not help, create a GitHub issue at https://github.com/rage/tmc-vscode/issues and attach the details that **Copy Details** and **TestMyCode: Show Logs** give you.
