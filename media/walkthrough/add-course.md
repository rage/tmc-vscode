# Add Course

**Add Course** lists the courses you are enrolled in on courses.mooc.fi first, then the organizations on tmc.mooc.fi, whose courses are listed under them. Search narrows both lists.

The course you add opens in its **course home**, which lists its exercises with your points and deadlines, says when there are new exercises or updates, and downloads them. Your courses also appear in the **Courses** view in the TestMyCode sidebar; clicking one opens its course home again.

Not sure which course to pick? Your course material names it.
