## Data collected by the extension

The extension has no trackers or telemetry. It is open source, and anyone can check what it does at [github.com/rage/tmc-vscode](https://github.com/rage/tmc-vscode).

When you submit an exercise for grading, the extension sends our server that exercise's folder, which holds only your solution, and the language for error messages, which is your computer's locale. We may check submissions for plagiarism, and we may use the IP address that sent one to block spam and prevent abuse.

The same applies when you share your solution with other students via the tmc.mooc.fi or courses.mooc.fi paste.

Every request to our server, such as logging in, downloading or submitting, carries the version of this extension. We use it to block outdated and misbehaving versions.
