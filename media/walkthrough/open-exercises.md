# Download and open exercises

Each course has its own workspace, a VS Code window that holds its downloaded exercises.

- The **Courses** view in the TestMyCode sidebar lists the course's exercises by part. **Download All Exercises** on the course gets every one; select an exercise to download it and open its code.
- **Open Course Workspace** opens the course's window. Its exercises are folders in the Explorer.
- After a passing submission, **Next** in the result, or **TestMyCode: Open Next Exercise**, opens the next exercise to do.

The first time you open a course workspace, VS Code asks whether you trust its files, or opens it in Restricted Mode. Choose **Trust**: TestMyCode needs a trusted workspace to run tests and submit. The one decision covers all your courses and every exercise you download later.

When the course publishes new or updated exercises, TestMyCode offers to download them.
