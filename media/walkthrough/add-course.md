# Add a course

**Add Course** lists the courses you are enrolled in on courses.mooc.fi first, then the organizations on tmc.mooc.fi.

The course you add opens in **Course Details**, which lists its exercises with your points and deadlines, says when there are new exercises or updates, and downloads them. Your courses also appear in the **Courses** view in the TestMyCode sidebar; clicking one opens its Course Details again.

Not sure which course to pick? Your course material names it.
